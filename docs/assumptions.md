# Assumptions and their consequences

These are the material assumptions and deliberate choices in the current implementation. A check can verify a rule without establishing its scientific truth. Terms are defined in the [glossary](glossary.md); source details and access limits are in the [reference audit](reference-audit.json).

| Assumption or choice | Rationale and evidence | What is checked or remains untested | Consequence if it fails or is generalized |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The retained panel can answer a bounded annotation-retrieval question. | Eight source annotation categories were chosen before scoring; seven meet the minimum after filtering. This is a deliberate sample, not all possible mechanisms. | Selection and exclusions are in config.json and audit tables. | Results are conditional on this panel and do not estimate general drug-target recognition. |
| The supplied annotations are useful comparison labels. | Way et al. (2022) used mechanism annotations but described their limitations. They are not activities measured by Cell Painting. | Conflicting labels within an identity are excluded; label truth is not experimentally rechecked. | Incorrect, broad or context-dependent labels affect both apparent successes and failures. |
| The selected dose category is comparable enough for this restricted analysis. | A single recorded dose category avoids pooling several nominal treatment levels. The raw concentration column name conflicts with the paper’s units. | Original values are retained; no unit conversion is inferred. | Absolute concentrations and exposure equivalence remain unresolved; no potency claim follows. |
| InChIKey14 is an appropriate grouping key for avoiding exact connectivity reuse. | Matching supplied connectivity keys stay in one split. This is a grouping convention, not proof of statistical independence. | Grouping and split separation are checked; broader chemical-family separation is not. | Related structures, shared targets and technical contexts can still connect train and test. |
| Plate-local negative controls are available at analysis time. | Each plate is normalized using DMSO wells, including plates contributing test profiles. This reflects the declared measurement setting. | At least 12 controls per plate are required. Treatment rows do not determine their control center or scale. | This is not prediction on a plate lacking controls, nor a test of completely unseen experimental preparation. |
| Robust centering and scaling are useful summaries. | A median is resistant to individual extremes; 1.4826 × MAD has standard-deviation scale under a normal reference distribution. Normality is not established for every feature. | Missing or effectively zero scales become missing; pooled controls provide diagnostics only. | Normalization can leave technical bias and can change biological comparisons. |
| A featurewise replicate median is an adequate group summary. | It reduces individual-well influence and keeps each identity to one vector. Three distinct replicate plates and at most 5% missing normalized entries are required. | Replicate counts and missingness are exported. These thresholds are engineering choices, not literature-validated optimal values. | Within-group variation and subpopulation mixtures are not modeled. |
| The 72 selected features are sufficient for a compact demonstration. | Nine shape features plus three intensity summaries across five channels and three compartments reduce size while retaining interpretable inputs. | The panel is fixed before scoring. Training-only checks remove unusable columns. | It is not a reproduction of the full 1,781-feature source analysis or proof of an optimal representation. |
| Training-derived preprocessing and a fixed class-stratified split serve the intended comparison. | Feature filtering, median imputation and scaling fit training groups only. Validation selects settings from small fixed grids; no final refit uses its labels. | Code and tests check separation; the 48/14/14 assignment is exported. | Test results are now public and exploratory for future development. One split cannot quantify split-selection uncertainty. |
| Cosine similarity is useful for annotation retrieval. | It compares directions after declared preprocessing and ignores vector magnitude. Identity and shared plate-map references are excluded. | Zero vectors and absent eligible positives are handled explicitly. | A match is an association; an ineligible query does not test the biological hypothesis. |
| Uniform random rankings give an informative chance reference. | The expected AP formula conditions on each gallery’s size and number of annotated positives. | The implementation has small-case checks; deterministic top-1 tie ordering differs from scikit-learn’s score-grouped AP handling. | This expectation is not a biological null test or a significance level. |
| The fixed test groups can be resampled as exchangeable units for descriptive intervals. | A 2,000-resample percentile bootstrap conveys conditional small-sample variation. | Exchangeability is assumed, not demonstrated; shared chemistry and experimental structure remain. | Intervals omit fitting, split choice, measurement, annotation and dependence uncertainty. |
| Pinned inputs and software identify the calculation. | Checksums, source commit and dependency versions make reproduction inspectable. The source pipeline’s underlying measurements are accepted as published. | Input integrity and numerical reproduction are checked. | Hash agreement does not authenticate labels, correct upstream errors or establish clinical use. |

The existing [methods](methods.md) and [practical-use guide](practical-use.md) define operational behavior. These assumptions do not add new experiments or change the preserved results.
